# Yogi Sathi (साथी)

## New in v7
* **🆘 SOS tile**: a big red button that calls the chosen emergency person after a countdown. Choose who in Settings → Phone.
* **🔊 Loud tile**: sets the ringtone, media and call volume to full in one tap.
* **Speaking language**: Nepali or English, for the talking clock.
* **Hourly talking clock** (optional): says the time every hour from 7 am to 9 pm.
* **Double-tap to turn the screen off** (optional): double-tap the clock or an empty space. Android asks once to allow it.

## New in v2
* **Built-in phone**: family photos, 3 per row (adjustable). Tap a photo to call, with a 3-second countdown and Cancel; or choose "ask first" or "call at once". Add people from the phone's contacts or by typing; choose photos; reorder. Emergency 100/101/102 and a dial pad are included. Yogi Ecall is no longer needed.
* **Choose the tiles**: add any app (YouTube, Viber...) or the built-in Phone, Torch, Wi-Fi, Camera and Photos tiles. Remove or move them, and set 1 to 4 tiles per row.
* **Wi-Fi tile**: on Android 10 and newer it opens the Wi-Fi switch panel; on older phones it switches Wi-Fi directly.
* **A smaller clock** that leaves room for the tiles. Tap it to hear the time in **Nepali, 12-hour** ("बिहानको नौ बजेर पन्ध्र मिनेट गयो"), plus the date and **tithi**. It uses the Nepali voice, or a Hindi voice when there's no Nepali one.
* **Battery saving**: a true black screen, the clock updates once a minute only while it's showing, and the calendar is worked out once a day.

**A simple, friendly home screen for grandparents.** No ads, no account, works offline.

* **A huge clock**, with the time of day (बिहान, दिउँसो, साँझ or राति).
* **The Nepali date** (Bikram Sambat), the weekday, the AD date, the **tithi**, and **today's festival**.
* **Tap the clock to hear the time and date spoken**: in Nepali when the phone has a Nepali voice, otherwise in English.
* **Battery level**, with a reminder to charge when it gets low.
* **Big buttons** (two per row):
  * Call (opens Yogi Ecall), Videos (Amma), Medicines (Yogi Meds), Photos (Yogi Photos), WhatsApp, Camera and Calendar (Yogi Patro)
  * a **Torch** button
  * if an app isn't installed, it offers Yogi Store
* **Dims the screen at night.**
* **Family setup** (hold the gear for 2 seconds):
  * choose which buttons show
  * Nepali or English, and Nepali or Western digits
* **Can be the phone's home screen**, so Back can't get anyone lost.
