# Yogi Sathi (साथी)

<img src="icon_preview.png" width="120" align="right" />

**A simple, friendly home screen for grandparents.** No ads, no account, works offline.

* **A huge clock**, with the time of day (बिहान, दिउँसो, साँझ or राति).
* **The Nepali date** (Bikram Sambat), the weekday, the AD date, the **tithi**, and **today's festival**.
* **Tap the clock to hear the time and date spoken**: in Nepali when the phone has a Nepali voice, otherwise in English.
* **Battery level**, with a reminder to charge when it gets low.
* **Big buttons** (two per row):
  * Call (opens Yogi Ecall), Videos (Amma), Medicines (Yogi Meds), Photos (Yogi Photos), WhatsApp, Camera and Calendar (Yogi Patro)
  * a **Torch** button
  * if an app isn't installed, it offers Yogi Store
* **Dims the screen at night.**
* **Family setup** (hold the gear for 2 seconds):
  * choose which buttons show
  * Nepali or English, and Nepali or Western digits
* **Can be the phone's home screen**, so Back can't get anyone lost.
